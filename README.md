Move Five Shipping
Moving, Relocation & Logistics · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Company Overview
Official company name: Move Five Shipping / Move Five Sea Shipping Co. LLC.

Industry
Transportation, moving, relocation, shipping and logistics.

Move Five Shipping is a Dubai-based moving and logistics provider offering residential and commercial relocation, international moving, cargo transportation and storage solutions.

The company positions itself as an integrated relocation provider, combining multiple moving and shipping requirements under one operation.

Its website reports:
- 7+ years of experience.
- 8,500+ successful moves.
- 20+ moving trucks.
- 10,000+ sq. ft. storage facility.
- Licensed and insured operations.
  
These are company-published figures rather than independently audited statistics.
